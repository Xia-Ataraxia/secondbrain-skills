# Conversations

Identify the selected conversation and its turn range before extraction. Keep the selected range, speaker labels, timestamps and original turn order exactly. Do not invent missing labels or infer deleted messages. Mark inaccessible, collapsed or truncated turns in place as explicit omissions (`[omitted: {what} — {why}]`) and in the member's capture status.

An existing public locator is provenance, not permission to create a new share link. Never create a share link, upload the conversation or send it to a service as a capture step. Browser availability and successful extraction are observed separately; record the method that actually produced the text.

`captureMode`: `transcript` when the requested range was obtained, `curated-excerpts` for owner-chosen excerpts, `manifest-only` when only the locator or message list is available, `mixed` when members differ. A summary belongs in Agent Capture Notes, never in Original Content.

Read the destination's conversation/capture template when one is selected and preserve its declared engine and required metadata; do not substitute an ingest receipt or work report for the transcript. If its fields cannot express the capture status or omissions, report that gap rather than altering the template.
